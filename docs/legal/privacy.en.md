# AI Translator Privacy Policy

**Version 1.15, effective 6 November 2026. If this English translation and the Vietnamese version differ, the Vietnamese version prevails.**

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

AI Translator (the "app") is developed and operated by **Do Tien Phong** (an individual) ("we", "us"). Contact for privacy and personal-data matters: **support@aitranslator.vn**.

## 2. Summary

- Audio (computer audio and, if you choose, the microphone) is processed **entirely on your computer**. Audio is **never** sent anywhere and is not written to disk. When the source includes the microphone (a new install is preselected to **Computer audio + Microphone**), the app captures the microphone for the whole translation session, **even if you have muted yourself in the meeting app** (section 3).
- Text you type in **Type & Translate** (a box for writing a message, translating it and pasting it into another app) is also translated **entirely on your computer**: it is not sent anywhere and not written to disk (section 3).
- **Suggesting translation when a meeting starts** (Windows; on by default, turn it off in Settings › General): the app checks **on your computer** whether Teams, Zoom or Google Meet is in a meeting and playing sound, to show a "Start translating?" prompt. Nothing is stored, written to logs or sent anywhere, and translation never starts until you click.
- **Telling speakers apart** (only on macOS for now, Windows does not have this feature yet; on by default, turn it off in Settings › Subtitles › Speakers): the app briefly analyzes voice features on your computer to know which sentences come from the same person, and shows “Speaker 1”, “Speaker 2”. The voice features **live only in RAM, are deleted automatically when the session ends, are never written to disk and never leave your computer**, and the app does not use them to identify anyone (section 3). On macOS the app also reads **the names of participants shown in the Microsoft Teams desktop meeting window** to put names on the subtitles (section 3).
- **The Free plan (trial) requires Sign in with Google**; a paid plan that is still valid needs no sign-in. When you sign in, our server stores your **Google email** and your **Google account identifier** to manage your account, keys and trial and to contact you about the service; the email is currently not used for marketing. You sign out or delete the account in Settings › Account (section 15).
- The app has **no advertising and no analytics**. When it hits an error, it **automatically sends us an error report with personal data scrubbed out** so we can fix the problem; you can turn this off at any time (section 10).
- **Cloud models you turn on yourself** (off by default): if you enter your own API key for ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google) or DeepSeek and choose it, the app sends the **text** to translate, or the transcript text for meeting notes, straight from your computer to that provider using your key. **Audio is never sent**, and we neither receive nor store that text (section 16). Meeting notes made with the on-device model send nothing.
- The **website** aitranslator.vn measures visits with Google Analytics only **after you click "Accept"** on the notice bar. If you do not accept, the website does not load Google Analytics and sets no measurement cookies (section 14). The app does not use Google Analytics.
- If you write to us in the **support chat** (on the website or in the app), your messages are stored on our server, forwarded to the operator's Zalo account so they can reply, and **deleted automatically 90 days** after the last message; you can delete them sooner yourself (section 11).
- Our servers only store your **email** (when you buy a plan, or when you choose to leave it in the support chat), your **Google email and Google account identifier** (when you sign in, section 15), order details, your license, a hashed machine identifier, error reports (kept for 30 days), name-reader status reports (kept 60 days, with no participant names) and support chat messages (kept for 90 days), in order to issue keys, check licenses, make sure each machine and each Google account gets the Free trial only once, fix bugs and reply to you.
- Personal data on our servers is **kept until you ask us to delete it** (section 7) or delete your Google account in the app yourself (section 15), except error reports, which are deleted automatically after 30 days, and name-reader status reports after 60 days (section 10), and support chat messages, which are deleted automatically 90 days after the last message (section 11).

On macOS, Type & Translate can ask for the **Accessibility** permission to paste the translation into the box you are typing in. The app uses this permission only to send the paste keys (⌘V); it does not read text, content or the clipboard of other apps. If you do not grant it, the app only copies the translation to the clipboard for you to paste.

Also on macOS, showing speaker names uses the **Accessibility** permission to read the names of participants in the Microsoft Teams meeting window. The app reads only the Teams meeting window (no other window or app), takes only the display name, microphone state and “speaking” marker, and clicks or types nothing in Teams. To read it, the app turns on the accessibility mode of Teams while you translate a meeting; **the accessibility mode of Teams stays on until Teams is restarted** (it costs nothing while nothing reads it). If you do not grant the permission, or turn off “Get names from Teams” in Settings › Subtitles › Speakers, the app still tells speakers apart with colors and the labels “Speaker 1”, “Speaker 2”. After each app update macOS may ask you to grant Accessibility again. Windows does not have speaker separation or name reading yet.

The app uses the microphone only when the source is **Microphone** or **Computer audio + Microphone** (Settings › Audio). **A new install is preselected to Computer audio + Microphone**, so the app uses the microphone from the first translation session; you can switch to **Computer audio** (which does not open the microphone) in Settings › Audio at any time, and people who installed earlier keep the source they already chose. When the source includes the microphone, macOS asks for the **Microphone** permission; on Windows, the app's use of the microphone follows Windows' microphone privacy settings. While a translation session runs, the app captures the microphone continuously, **even if you have muted yourself in the meeting app**: muting there only stops your voice from reaching the other participants, not this app. The operating system shows when an app is using the microphone (on macOS, an orange dot in the menu bar). To stop the app capturing the microphone, press Stop. The microphone can pick up the voices of other people near you; ask their permission where needed (EULA section 6).

## 4. When the app uses the network

The app connects to the network only to:
1. **Download models** (including the speaker model, about 26 MB, when the feature is on) and check for new model versions, and **download the name-reading recipe** (a small description file with a digital signature; when the app opens and every 6 hours, only when the feature is on), all from `releases.aitranslator.vn`. These are file download requests only; none of your data is sent.
2. **Check for app updates** (from `releases.aitranslator.vn`).
3. **Talk to our license server** (`api.aitranslator.vn`): when you **Sign in with Google** (section 15), when registering the Free trial (right after you sign in; this sends a hash of the machine ID with the account token), when linking this computer's license to your Google account, when you sign out or delete the account, when you buy a plan, activate or deactivate, recover a key, check your license periodically, send error reports (section 10), send name-reader status reports (section 10) and (rarely) ask for the server's time when your computer's clock looks wrong. Asking for the time sends none of your data. When you sign in, the app asks this server to create a sign-in attempt and then opens Google's sign-in page (`accounts.google.com`) in your browser; your browser, not the app, connects to Google. While it is translating, the app does not send any sign-in or account request. When you use the **support chat**, the app also sends and receives chat messages through this server (section 11): only while the Support screen is open, or while you are waiting for a reply to a message you sent in the last 24 hours and the app is not translating; if you have never used the chat, the app makes no connection for it. While translating, the app sends or receives chat messages only when the Support screen is open or when you press send.
4. **The payment page** provided by PayOS (VietQR bank transfer) or Creem (international card), opened in your browser when you buy a plan.
5. **Our website** `aitranslator.vn`, opened in your browser when you click a link in the app.
6. **Call a cloud model you turn on yourself** (section 16), only after you have entered an API key and chosen it: `api.openai.com` (ChatGPT), `generativelanguage.googleapis.com` (Gemini) or `api.deepseek.com` (DeepSeek), each time only the provider you chose. The app calls it straight from your computer with your key, not through our servers, and sends the text to translate or the transcript text for notes; it does not send audio.

App versions before 0.2.7 connect to the same servers under our former domain `aitranslator.io.vn`; the website at the former domain redirects to `aitranslator.vn`.

There are no other connections, and none carries audio (including microphone audio) or text you type in Type & Translate. Transcript text or translations leave your computer only through connection 6, when you turn on a cloud model yourself.

## 7. Retention and your rights

**Personal data on our servers is kept until you ask us to delete it.** You can also delete your Google account in the app yourself (section 15). We do not delete it automatically, except error reports (deleted after 30 days, and name-reader status reports after 60 days, section 10) and the support chat (deleted 90 days after the last message, section 11).

You have the right to know what is stored, to request **deletion** or **anonymization**, to withdraw consent, and to complain under Vietnam's **Law on Personal Data Protection**. Send requests to **support@aitranslator.vn** from the email you used when buying, the Google email you sign in with, or the email you left in the support chat (so we can confirm it is you). We respond within the period required by law.

When you ask for deletion we will:
- **remove** your email and computer name, and the interface language stored on your orders;
- **delete** the error reports of the machines that ever activated a key for that email (section 10), and the name-reader status reports of those same machines;
- **remove** that email from the Google account signed in with it and unlink the account from license and trial, as when you delete the account yourself (section 15, which lists what the server keeps);
- **delete** the support chats in which you left that email, both on our server and the copies forwarded into the operator's Zalo conversation (section 11);
- **keep** the hash of the machine ID (pseudonymous data, only to prevent abuse: one machine per key, the temporary lock for excessive machine switching, reusing the correct activation when you re-activate the same machine, one Free trial per machine);
- **keep** the time you consented to email processing (proof of earlier consent);
- **keep** the order row at the level accounting requires: order code, date, amount (and currency), the payment gateway's session ID, and for auto-renewing orders Creem's subscription ID and the end date of the period collected (these rows hold no email).

After deletion your license still works but **can no longer be recovered by email**. Data on your computer (history, settings) is deleted by you in the app ("Delete models and data") or by uninstalling.

Self-expiring technical data (rate-limit counters; Cloudflare logs) and Resend's mail logs are outside our deletion; their retention follows those parties' policies. Data you enter on Creem's page and the transaction records kept by Creem (as the merchant of record it must keep tax records) are outside a deletion request sent to us; to have them deleted at Creem, contact Creem. Error reports from a machine that never activated a key are not tied to any email, so they cannot be found by email, and the same goes for the name-reader status reports of that machine; those error reports are deleted automatically after 30 days and the status reports after 60 days. A support chat in which you left no email cannot be found by email either: delete it yourself with the button in the chat, or send a request with the approximate time and content of your messages so we can find it; otherwise it is deleted automatically 90 days after the last message (the copy forwarded into Zalo is deleted only when you ask).

## 8. Children

The service is for people aged **16 or over**. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16; if you are a parent or guardian and believe your child gave us an email, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

Token-signing and payment keys live only on our servers (as secrets) and are not in the app. Connections to our servers use HTTPS. Administrative access is protected by a separate login. No system is perfectly secure; if an incident affects personal data we will notify as required by law.

## 10. Error reports

When the app hits an error, it **automatically sends us an error report** through our license server (`api.aitranslator.vn`) so we can find and fix the problem. This is **on by default**: the Terms step the first time you open the app has a pre-ticked box you can untick, and people who installed the app earlier have it on after updating to a version that includes this feature. You can turn it off or on again at any time in **Settings › Privacy › Send error reports automatically**; turning it off deletes any reports still waiting to be sent. You can also send a report yourself at any time with the **Send log to support** button on the About screen: it sends immediately, even if you turned automatic sending off, and gives you a **report code** to hand to support.

| What | Details |
|---|---|
| When it is sent automatically | When the app's previous run ended abruptly; when a translation session stops because of an error in the app (not errors caused by you or your environment, such as a missing recording or microphone permission, a microphone that cannot be opened, a used-up quota, an ended trial, no network or no disk space); or when one of the app's helper programs dies and is restarted automatically. A report is only sent while the app is not translating, and each machine sends at most 5 reports a day |
| The report contains | The report code, the type and code of the error, the time it happened; the app version, the operating system and its version, the architecture, the chip or CPU name, RAM, the type of GPU in use, the model pack, the name of your license plan (never the key), the interface language; a **hash of the machine ID**; and the part of the log that relates to the error (at most 64 KB) |
| The report does not contain | Audio (including microphone audio), transcripts, translations, text typed in Type & Translate, license keys, tokens, email addresses, the computer name, the microphone name, names of meeting participants or speakers, the operating-system user name, IP addresses |
| Scrubbing | Before sending, the app removes personal folder paths, keys, tokens and email addresses from the log; if any sensitive pattern is left, the whole report is dropped. Our server checks again and rejects a report if anything is left |
| How it is tied to you | The machine-ID hash in the report is the same one we store for licensing and the Free trial (section 5), so we **can link a report to that machine's license and email** when handling a support request |
| How long it is kept | At most **30 days**, then deleted automatically; the server keeps at most 3,000 reports and deletes the oldest first |
| Who can see it | Only the operator of the app, through an admin page with its own login. The data is held on Cloudflare (section 6) |

- We use reports only to find and fix bugs; we do not use them for advertising, sell them or share them with anyone other than the processors in section 6.
- To have the reports of your machine deleted before the 30 days are up, ask for deletion under section 7: when we handle a deletion request by email, we also delete the error reports of the machines that ever activated a key for that email.
- If you would rather send nothing, untick the box at the Terms step or turn the switch off in Settings; the app keeps working fully with it off.
- **Name-reader status report** (when the speaker feature is on and the switch for error reports is on): each day, each machine sends at most one report per meeting app and its version, through the same server, so that we know when Teams changes its interface and the reader can no longer read. The report contains only: the date (UTC) of the figures; the app version, the build type (release or test build), the operating system, its version and the CPU architecture; the name and version of the meeting app; the recipe version; the reading status (names read, no names seen yet, nobody seen speaking, permission missing, blocked); the total meeting minutes and the minutes in which names were read; the number of indicator patterns the app learned by itself; a description of the kind of interface element of those indicators (role and sub-role, numeric position in the interface tree, class; no participant names or text); a flag saying whether the report comes from our own automated check or from a user's machine; and the hashed machine ID as in error reports; **no participant names and no text from the meeting**. Our server also records the time it received the report. It is kept **60 days**, then deleted automatically; turning the error-report switch off also deletes any figures waiting to be sent; a deletion request by email (section 7) also deletes these reports of the machines that ever activated a key for that email.

## 16. Cloud models and meeting notes

This section covers the use of third-party models **only when you turn them on yourself**: translating with a cloud model (which you choose in Settings) and making Meeting notes with a cloud model. **Both are off by default**, and the app sends no content out.

- **To turn it on:** you enter your own API key for ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google) or DeepSeek, then choose that provider. The key is kept in the operating system's key store (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager); the app does not send the key to us and does not write it to logs.
- **When translating:** each sentence being translated is sent to the provider you chose. It may come with the terms from your glossary that appear in that sentence or, if you turn on the experimental translate-with-context option, the sentence before it.
- **When making notes:** the text of each sentence in the session (the translation into the language you chose, or the original sentence if it has not been translated), with its sequence number and the speaker's label or name if there is one (including a participant's name if the app was able to read it), plus the app's fixed instructions on how to make the notes. Your glossary is not sent this time.
- **Never sent:** audio (including the microphone); your email, license or account; text you type in Type & Translate.
- **Consent:** for meeting notes, the first time you use a cloud model the app shows a dialog naming the provider and sends only after you agree; if you switch to another provider it asks again.
- **Turning it off:** choose the on-device model again (in the translation part of Settings, and on the Notes screen) and the app stops sending immediately. Notes made with the on-device model send nothing.
- **On the provider's side:** they receive the content straight from your computer and process it under their terms, your account type and your data settings with them (section 6). Whether they keep or use that content is up to them; read their policy before you turn it on. We receive no copy.
